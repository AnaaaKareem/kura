Ways to add a passkey

When you click Add a passkey, your browser lets you choose one of these options:

  • Windows Hello (face recognition, fingerprint, or PIN)
  • Apple Passwords / iCloud Keychain (Touch ID or Face ID)
  • Google Password Manager or another compatible password manager
  • Your phone, by scanning the QR code shown by your browser
  • Physical security key (such as a YubiKey, via USB or NFC)
