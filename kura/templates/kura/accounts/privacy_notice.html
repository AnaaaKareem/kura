Data Protection

Privacy notice

How Kura processes personal and academic records under GDPR, Egyptian Law No. 151 of 2020, Law No. 175 of 2018, and FERPA.

1. Data we collect and lawful basis

Kura processes university identity details, enrollments, attendance, assessment submissions, released grades, and security audit logs to deliver coursework, verify academic integrity, and protect accounts.

  • Account and academic delivery (enrollments, coursework, grading, transcripts): institutional contract and public-interest educational task.
  • Authentication, MFA, and security logs: legal obligation and security interest.
  • Breached-password verification: k-anonymity lookup sending only the first 5 SHA-1 hash characters to HaveIBeenPwned (no full hash or password leaves the server).

2. Retention schedules

  • Security logs and database IP addresses: retained 180 days (IPs masked to /24 or /48 subnets after 180 days).
  • Sent notification emails: bodies wiped upon delivery/failure; sent rows purged after 30 days.
  • Quarantined malware uploads: purged after 7 days.
  • Academic records (enrollments, grades, grade history, transcripts, submitted attempts): retained for the institutional record lifetime.

3. Your rights

  • Access & portability: Download a JSON copy of your own account and released academic records from Privacy & data.
  • Restriction of processing: Restrict optional processing (such as due-date reminder emails) at any time.
  • Erasure with academic-record exceptions: Request erasure of non-exempt auxiliary metadata (session history, pending reset tokens, optional office room). Statutory security logs and academic/grading records remain retained as required by law and institutional policy.
